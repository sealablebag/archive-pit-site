ARCHIVE PIT

PRIVACY POLICY

last updated october 22, 2026

Archive Pit runs only on your own PC. It has no servers. It doesn't collect, sell or share any of your data.

WHAT IT USES FROM GOOGLE

Only after you sign in and allow it:

  • YouTube upload (youtube.upload): uploads the recordings you pick, or the ones your settings send, to your channel.
  • YouTube read-only (youtube.readonly): reads your channel name and whether the channel can take videos over 15 minutes.
  • Google Drive (drive.file), only if you turn on Drive backup: uploads backups. It can only see files it made itself.

WHERE YOUR DATA GOES

Videos and backups go straight from your PC to Google. Your Google sign-in is stored only in Windows Credential Manager on your PC. Your recordings stay on your drive. Archive Pit never deletes them without asking.

REMOVING ACCESS

Press Disconnect in Archive Pit, which revokes access with Google and deletes the sign-in. You can also remove it at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

GOOGLE AND YOUTUBE

Archive Pit's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Uploading to YouTube through Archive Pit means you're also using YouTube under the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
